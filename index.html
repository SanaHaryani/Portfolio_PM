01 / About

Bringing structure
to ambiguity.

I’m a project manager who works where business meets technology. At IAV I supported HR-IT systems and served as Interim Product Owner for a cross-functional Roles & Permissions initiative. Before that, I managed client projects end to end — clients, designers, developers and vendors — building on the project-management foundation from my B.Sc. With an M.Sc. in Computer Science, I understand both the business and the tech side, and I turn requirements into work teams can actually deliver.
